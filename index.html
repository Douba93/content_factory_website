AI-assisted creator workflow

Turn your long videos into ready-to-publish short clips.

Content Factory helps creators identify engaging moments in their own videos, generate vertical clips, add subtitles, prepare captions and hashtags, and organize content for publishing to supported social platforms.

WORKFLOW

From source video to short-form content

01

Import

Provide a supported source video that you own or are authorized to use.

02

Analyze

AI-assisted analysis identifies candidate moments from the transcript and timing information.

03

Create

Selected moments are rendered as vertical clips with subtitles and platform-ready formatting.

04

Prepare & publish

Content Factory creates captions and hashtags and can send approved content through supported platform integrations.

FEATURES

A focused workflow for creators

Automatic clipping

Find useful moments without manually scrubbing through an entire long-form video.

Vertical video

Prepare short-form output designed for mobile-first social platforms.

Dynamic subtitles

Generate timed subtitles from the source audio for accessible, watchable clips.

Social metadata

Generate a clip-specific caption and relevant hashtags based on its actual content.

Publishing queue

Organize approved clips into a persistent queue instead of publishing everything at once.

User-controlled publishing

Publishing is opt-in and requires the creator to connect and authorize their own account.

Built around creator-owned content

Content Factory is intended for creators processing videos they own or have permission to use. Platform connections require authorization by the account owner.
