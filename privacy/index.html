Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Overview

Content Factory is a tool that helps creators process their own video content into short-form clips, generate supporting metadata, and connect to supported publishing platforms. This policy explains the information that may be processed when the service is used.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features used, Content Factory may process source videos, audio, transcripts, generated clips, captions, hashtags, processing metadata, publishing status, and technical logs. When a user connects a third-party platform such as TikTok, authorization information and tokens required for that integration may also be processed.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide requested functionality, including importing and processing media, generating clips and metadata, authenticating connected accounts, preparing publishing queues, publishing user-approved content, diagnosing errors, and maintaining service security and reliability.

4. TikTok integration

When a user chooses to connect TikTok, Content Factory uses the permissions authorized by that user to provide the requested integration. Content Factory does not require users to provide their TikTok password directly. Users can revoke access through applicable TikTok account or developer controls.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers or connected platforms only when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user, comply with law, or protect the service and its users.

6. Retention and security

Data is retained only as reasonably necessary for the operation of the service, troubleshooting, legal obligations, or as configured by the user. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored information, although no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. User choices

Users may choose not to connect a social account and may use generation features without publishing where supported. Users may revoke connected-platform authorization using the controls provided by the relevant platform.

8. Children's privacy

Content Factory is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the applicable service or connected social platform.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Content Factory evolves. The date above identifies the latest version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of Content Factory through the contact information provided with the deployed service.
